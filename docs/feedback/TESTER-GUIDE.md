# Play-test CurioTrail: a guide for friends

Thanks for helping! CurioTrail is an early prototype of a learning game set on a Viking island. This round is about one new chapter, **The Merchant's Ledger**, where you help a market trader fix her spreadsheet ledger, then face a tax collector's audit.

**Preview link:** _to be added once the preview is live and checked._ Please use only this link, not the main CurioTrail site.

You don't need an account, and nothing to install. It works on a phone or a computer, in a normal browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox or Edge). Your progress is saved only in your own browser.

## What to do (about 30–40 minutes)

1. **Open the preview link.** Choose **Explore the map**.
2. **Play "The Missing Signal"** in the Signal Woods (about 10 minutes). It opens the next part of the island. If it asks for a name, any nickname is fine.
3. **Go to the Trading Market** on the map and start **The Merchant's Ledger**.
4. **Play it to the end, including the audit.** There are no wrong ways to play. If you get stuck, use what the game offers (hints, the 📖 Codex, "Show me the answer").
5. **Look at your results**, then check on the map whether the Great Hall has opened.
6. **Fill in the feedback form** (`FEEDBACK-TEMPLATE.md`) and send it back (see below).

Please play as you naturally would, and try not to ask anyone for help while playing. Where you got stuck is exactly what we want to learn.

## Good to know

- **Phone:** during a round, the bar with the rune stones, XP and the Codex stays at the top. Scroll up to see the site menu again.
- **The 3D island** may be slow on older devices. Under *Progress & settings*, set **3D graphics** to **Light** or **Off** (Off shows a flat map; everything still works).
- **Start again:** *Progress & settings* → **Reset all progress**, then **Yes, reset everything**.
- **Silver coins** are an optional side-bet. They never change your score or medal.

## Sending feedback

Any of these works:

- copy the form into a message or email to the person who invited you;
- or, if you have a GitHub account, paste it as a comment on the pull request they sent you.

Screenshots of anything odd are very welcome.

## Privacy

The game stores your progress only in your browser on your device. It sends nothing anywhere. Please leave personal details out of your feedback.
