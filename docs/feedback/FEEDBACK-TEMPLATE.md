# CurioTrail feedback form

Thank you for playing! Copy this form into a message, an email or a comment on the pull request, and fill in what you can. Short answers are fine. Skip anything you'd rather not answer.

Please don't include personal details such as your full name, email or location. We don't need them.

---

## About your session

- **Device:** (e.g. iPhone 13, Android phone, Windows laptop, MacBook)
- **Browser:** (e.g. Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
- **How long you played:** (about … minutes)
- **How far you got:** (e.g. finished The Missing Signal / reached Hrolf's audit / finished The Merchant's Ledger)
- **Spreadsheets before today:** never used them / used them a little / use them often

## 1. Finding your way (navigation)

- How easy was it to find what to do next? **1 (very hard) – 5 (very easy):**
- Was there a moment you didn't know where to click or tap? Where?
- Did anything on the screen get in the way (menus, buttons, the bar at the top)?

## 2. Understanding the game (gameplay clarity)

- How clear were the instructions in each round? **1 (very unclear) – 5 (very clear):**
- Was there a round you didn't understand? Which one, and what was confusing?
- When you got something wrong, did the game help you see why? (yes / partly / no) What would have helped?
- Did you notice the ✓ and ✗ marks in the ledger, or the market stall reacting?
- Did the medal at the end make sense to you?

## 3. Enjoyment

- How much did you enjoy it? **1 (not at all) – 5 (a lot):**
- What was the most fun moment?
- What was the most boring or frustrating moment?
- Would you play another chapter? (yes / maybe / no) Why?

## 4. Bugs and problems

For each problem, if you can:

- **What you were doing:** (e.g. "In the Rune Wheel, I pressed Spin")
- **What you expected:**
- **What happened instead:**
- **Did it happen again if you tried?** (yes / no / didn't try)
- **Screenshot:** (attach one if you can)

## 5. Accessibility and comfort

- Was the text easy to read (size, contrast)? Anything too small or faint?
- Did anything move or flash in a way that bothered you?
- Did you use a keyboard only, a screen reader, zoom, or larger text? If so, did everything work?
- On a phone: was anything hard to tap, cut off, or hidden behind the keyboard?

## 6. Suggestions

- If you could change one thing, what would it be?
- Anything else you'd like to tell us?

---
