island-high.glb and island-low.glb are based on "Viking Island - Minecraft"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/viking-island-minecraft-fd76e3824b7647d98be20d3e2160e018)
by cubical.xyz (https://sketchfab.com/cubical), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: merged into chunks, baked to flat per-face colours (the original
texture atlas is not included), water and seabed removed, simplified,
quantized and meshopt-compressed. See tools/optimize-island.mjs.
These two files are licensed CC-BY-4.0, not under the project's MIT licence.
