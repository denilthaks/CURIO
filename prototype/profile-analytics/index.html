    Period

    How these numbers are counted
    Attempt
    Each answer you submit. The first answer to a challenge is the initial attempt; every later answer to that challenge is a retry. So retries = attempts − initial attempts, and an initial attempt is never a retry.
    Challenges completed
    Challenges with a correct answer in the selected period and quest.
    Retry-to-success rate
    Challenges with at least one retry that were then completed ÷ challenges with at least one retry.
    Concepts revisited
    Different skills you came back to (in a practice round or by re-reading a lesson) on a later day than you first met them.
    Mastery score
    Items answered correctly ÷ items in a mastery check. Mastery change = latest − earliest score for each quest with at least two checks in the period, averaged across those quests, in percentage points.
    Recovery
    For each incorrect attempt, what happened next on the same challenge within days. Recovery rate = incorrect attempts followed by a correct next answer ÷ all incorrect attempts.
    Hints
    Each hint you ask for. Hints are not attempts and are never counted as one.

          Privacy and your data

          How CurioTrail remembers things

          There are two separate layers. Cookies and storage keep the game working and remember your choices. Learning events record what happens in challenges so this profile can show your progress. One isn’t the other, and each has its own settings.

          Layer 1

          Cookies & storage

          Remembers that you’re playing, your display preferences and the privacy choices on this page.

            Layer 2

            Learning events

            Challenge started, answer submitted, hint asked for, mastery check, concept revisited. No advertising cookies are needed for any of this.

              Privacy settings

              A. Cookies and storage

              Choose which optional kinds of storage CurioTrail may use. Each one is explained before you choose.

              Essential storage Always on

              Keeps a session going, saves your game progress in this browser and remembers these privacy choices. It can’t be switched off here, because turning it off would stop the game saving and some features would not work. Which items really count as essential will be checked in the privacy review.

              B. Learning events

              Your progress record

              To show this profile and save your place, CurioTrail records these events while you play:

              • ▸ Challenge started
              • ✎ Answer submitted (right or not yet)
              • 💡 Hint requested
              • ↻ Retry completed
              • ✓ Challenge completed
              • ◎ Mastery check passed (and its score)
              • ↺ Earlier concept revisited

              Each event has a time, the quest and the challenge. It does not include your real name, email address, location, device fingerprint or what you type outside the challenge.

              Turning this off stops new events being used to improve CurioTrail. It doesn’t delete events already recorded; see “Your data” below.
